=====================================================================
COINS FOR ANYTHING (coinsforanything.com) — DESIGN GUIDE
Reference file for building widgets, dashboards, and UI that match
the CFA brand. Derived from the live homepage (Aug 2026).
=====================================================================

BRAND PERSONALITY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Veteran-owned, military-rooted, patriotic, proud, hardworking.
- Craftsmanship + heritage: "The Quality is Always Here."
- Tone: confident, direct, a little bold/loud — not corporate-slick,
  not minimalist. Think workshop/mint, not tech startup.
- Trust signals matter: years in business, 20M+ coins minted,
  60,000+ designs, Inc. 5000, big-name clients (Coca-Cola, Ford).

COLOR PALETTE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Orange (CTA/accent):   #F58220  (approx; ranges #EF8122–#F6871F)
  - Used for: primary buttons, script accent words, links, section
    accents, "SIGN UP" callouts, hover states.
Navy Blue (secondary):         #2B3990  (approx; deep royal/navy)
  - Used for: phone/CTA panels, secondary buttons, small accents.
Near-Black (dark sections):    #1A1A1A – #222222
  - Used for: main nav bar, dark content bands, footer-adjacent
    sections, "Get a Free Quote" panels.
White:                         #FFFFFF
Off-white / light gray:        #F4F4F4 (footer + light section bg)
Body text gray:                #4A4A4A – #555555
Teal/steel-blue underline accent (subtle): #4A7C8C (thin rules under
  section headings — used sparingly)

Rule of thumb: white or wood-textured background, dark charcoal
sections for contrast bands, orange for every action. Orange is the
ONLY call-to-action color; navy is support, never the primary CTA.

TEXTURES & IMAGERY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Light wood-plank texture backgrounds on hero/feature sections
  (weathered whitewashed wood). Dark walnut wood for premium/dark
  bands. If no texture asset available, use warm off-white (#F5F1EA)
  or #1E1E1E as stand-ins.
- Photography: real product shots of coins on dark wood, coins held
  or displayed, denim/apparel flat lays. High contrast, warm.
- American flag motifs, eagles, military insignia appear in product
  imagery — don't add flags decoratively in UI; let product photos
  carry the patriotism.
- Mascot: "Coin Nerd" cartoon character (glasses, magnifying glass)
  used for newsletter/fun contexts only.

TYPOGRAPHY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Three-tier system:

1. HEADINGS — bold condensed/geometric sans, ALL CAPS, generous
   letter-spacing. Looks like Oswald / Bebas-adjacent / Montserrat
   Bold in caps. White on dark, charcoal on light.
   Example: "AMERICA'S ... OWNED CUSTOM COIN COMPANY"

2. SCRIPT ACCENT — a brush/marker script used for ONE emphasized
   word or phrase inside a heading, always in orange (or white on
   dark). Examples: "Largest Veteran", "Free", "Different!",
   "Sign Up", "Custom". Suggested web stands-ins: "Yellowtail",
   "Pacifico", "Caveat Bold", or similar brush script.
   USAGE RULE: max one script phrase per section. It's the brand's
   signature move — accent, never body.

3. BODY — clean humanist sans (Open Sans / Roboto / Lato family
   feel), 14–16px, ~1.6 line-height, gray #4A4A4A. Bold inline for
   key phrases ("100% veteran-owned").

Section headings pattern: centered, ALL CAPS, letterspaced, with a
thin horizontal rule on either side or a short underline beneath
(e.g. "SATISFIED CUSTOMERS", "LATEST BLOG POSTS").

BUTTONS & CTAs
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary:   Solid orange (#F58220), white ALL-CAPS bold label,
           trailing "›" chevron, rectangular (0–2px radius),
           generous horizontal padding. e.g. "GET A FREE QUOTE ›",
           "ORDER NOW ›", "VISIT BLOG ›".
Secondary: Solid near-black (#1A1A1A) with white caps label, same
           shape ("ORDER NOW", "VIEW ›").
Tertiary:  Navy (#2B3990) for phone/utility CTAs.
- Buttons are punchy and frequent — almost every section ends in one.
- No ghost/outline buttons, no pill shapes, no soft shadows.

LAYOUT PATTERNS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Full-width horizontal bands alternating: light/wood → dark →
  white → dark. Strong contrast between adjacent sections.
- Top utility bar: black strip with orange "SIGN UP!" teaser text.
- Sticky-style nav: black bar, white caps links, orange highlighted
  CTA item on the right ("GET A FREE QUOTE"), one inverted white
  item ("STORE").
- 3–4 column card grids for categories (Military Coins, Custom
  Coins, Badges, Displays): image card + small caps label beneath.
- Two-column text sections with left-aligned caps headings +
  short orange underline.
- Logo carousel for social proof (grayscale client logos, one
  colored on hover/active).
- Dark 3-up promo band (Ceramic Chips / Badges / Lapel Pins):
  white caps heading, short copy, stacked small CTAs.
- Footer: light gray, 4–5 columns of plain link lists, small caps
  column headings, contact block, social icons in orange circles.

VOICE & COPY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Superlatives + proof: "America's Largest Veteran Owned...",
  "Over 50,000 companies... TRUST US", numbers everywhere.
- Direct imperative CTAs: "Order Your T-Shirt Today", "Get a Free
  Quote", "Sign Up to Win Free Coins".
- Occasional playful energy ("Get a little geeky over challenge
  coins, too?") but never at the expense of pride/quality messaging.
- Key phrases to reuse: "The Quality is Always Here", "100%
  veteran owned and operated", "custom challenge coins".

WIDGET-BUILDING CHECKLIST (for Claude Code)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Orange #F58220 for all primary actions; never invent new accents.
[ ] Dark charcoal bands for contrast sections; wood texture or warm
    off-white for light sections.
[ ] Headings: bold condensed sans, ALL CAPS, letterspaced.
[ ] One brush-script accent word max per component, in orange.
[ ] Rectangular buttons, caps labels, trailing › chevron.
[ ] Body text: neutral sans, gray, comfortable line-height.
[ ] Include trust/proof elements where relevant (counts, badges).
[ ] No rounded pills, pastel palettes, thin-weight typography,
    glassmorphism, or gradients — none of these fit the brand.
[ ] Mobile-friendly: bands stack, grids collapse to 1–2 columns.
